# The Rat King — Pricing & Reward Tiers

**Core principle:** Release price is the anchor ($10). Kickstarter backers always pay *less* than release (their reward for early support + risk). Digital extras carry the higher tiers because they cost nothing to duplicate. Exactly one physical tier to keep fulfillment sane for a 2-person team. You can raise the price later as content grows.

---

## Kickstarter reward tiers

| Price | Tier name | What they get |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **$5** | Rat Recruit | Discord supporter role + name on the backer wall. *(No game — for people who just want to help. Discord + demo are free to everyone.)* |
| **$6** | Early Bird copy *(first 100 only)* | Full game at launch + Discord role + name in credits. Limited = launch-day urgency. |
| **$8** | Full game (standard) | Full game + Discord role + name in credits. Your core tier. |
| **$18** | Deluxe digital | Game + digital soundtrack + digital artbook/wallpapers + special Discord role. **The tier you want people to pick** — nearly pure margin. |
| **$30** | Physical fan pack | Everything above + sticker pack (+ optional small print/keychain). Priced to cover production + shipping + a little margin. One physical tier only. |
| **$55** | Rat King (superfan) | Everything + name an NPC/rat or in-game item + "producer" credit + exclusive Discord role. A few of these fund a big chunk of the campaign. |

**Discount logic:** Backers pay $6–8 vs. the $10 release price = a genuine ~20–40% early-supporter discount without devaluing the game.

**Pitch line:** *"Back now for $6 — it'll be $10+ on release."*

## Steam / release roadmap (for later)

| Stage | Price | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **Launch** | **$10** | Matches the small, linear, complete-once scope. Safe, appealing, no sticker shock. |
| **Launch week** | ~$9 (10% off) | A short launch discount is standard and boosts visibility on Steam's "new & trending." |
| **After major content update** (more weapons, extra floors) | **$12** | Raising price as you add value is normal and expected. |
| **If you add randomization / real replay value** | **$15** | Only justified once runs genuinely vary and replay time goes up. |

**Rules of thumb:**
- You can *raise* a Steam price over time as content grows — many indies launch cheap and climb. You generally shouldn't launch high and cut.
- Never make Kickstarter backers pay more than the eventual retail price.
- Let Steam handle regional pricing automatically (don't hand-set it).
- Keep sales/discounts modest early (10–20%); deep discounts train buyers to wait.

---

## If randomization ships before the Kickstarter

Bump everything up a notch: release $12–15, KS standard $10, early bird $8, and shift the digital/physical/superfan tiers up a few dollars each. The added replayability earns it.

---

## What actually sets the price

The demo, not this sheet. Once floor 1 + the first boss are genuinely fun and playable, you'll feel what it's worth — and so will your first players. Ship the demo, watch reactions, then lock the number. Pricing is easy to change; fun is the hard part.
